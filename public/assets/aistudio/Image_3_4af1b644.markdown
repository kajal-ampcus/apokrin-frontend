## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, elite enterprise consulting aesthetic tailored for C-suite decision-makers, VP of Engineering leaders, and procurement directors. The visual tone balances technical rigor, forward-looking AI transformation, and dependable enterprise reliability.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Glassmorphism and Precision Tech Dark-mode accents**. The interface delivers deep, confident spatial depth through rich obsidian and midnight navy backdrops punctuated by razor-sharp border strokes, glowing electric blue interactive anchors, and vivid cyan signifiers. Generous negative space and architectural typographic balance evoke institutional credibility while asserting innovation across cloud engineering, artificial intelligence, and global technical talent delivery.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for a dark-first environment that communicates stability and intellectual depth.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: The foundational enterprise tech blue. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and high-priority interactions.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: High-energy electric cyan. Reserved for forward-looking technological focal points such as AI milestones, active metrics, code highlights, and gradient terminal stops.
- **Tertiary (`#5558FF`)**: Digital indigo, providing a modern bridge between royal cobalt and deep backgrounds for specialized badges, hover highlights, and gradient fills.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19`)**: An ultra-deep space black-navy neutral, providing maximum contrast against both high-luminance text and electric accents.
- **Named Accents**: 
  - `navy-canvas` (`#0A1128`) and `surface-slate` (`#0F172A`): Foundational structural surface layers.
  - `surface-card` (`#111C38`): Distinct elevated platform for enterprise modules.
  - `surface-border` (`#1E293B`): Hairline boundary stroke for containers.
  - `accent-orange` (`#EA580C`): High-alert and urgency signaling, system notifications, or rapid staffing turnaround badges.
  - `text-muted` (`#94A3B8`): Balanced secondary hierarchy text meeting WCAG AA standards against midnight backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headings and display instances to deliver a clean, contemporary tech posture with subtle geometric precision. For body copy, data readouts, and interactive micro-copy, **Inter** provides utilitarian clarity, high legibility at dense resolutions, and strict optical neutrality.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em` on display sizes) with purposeful contrast between bold headline weights and adjacent descriptive copy.
- **Body & Data**: Generous line heights ensure cognitive ease when scanning complex architectures, SLA metrics, or cloud infrastructure descriptions.
- **Labels & Micro-text**: Uppercase tracking (`+0.05em`) is applied to small technical tags, status chips, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by maximum content boundaries of `1280px` for standard applications and `1440px` for high-density enterprise dashboard views.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and up to `5rem` (`80px`) external margins to enforce breathing room around key architectural content.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex analytical blocks reflow into 2-column or stacked vertical cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` margins. Multi-column grids compress to a linear single-column orientation.

Spacing tokens follow a strict 4px/8px modular rhythm where internal component padding relies exclusively on `space-xs` through `space-lg`, while layout sections mandate `space-xl` and `space-2xl` increments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **tonal surface stacking** reinforced by **low-contrast precision outlines** and faint ambient back-glows, bypassing traditional heavy drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (Canvas)**: `#0A1128` (Navy Canvas) or `#0B0F19` (Void).
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Containers & Panels)**: `#0F172A` with a 1px solid border in `#1E293B`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Interactive Cards)**: `#111C38` with a 1px translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns)**: `#152244` backed by a 16px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed with `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.24)`.
- **Luminous Depth (Hero & Featured Focal Points)**: Soft, non-distracting gradient radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12), transparent 70%)`) placed behind high-value proposition surfaces to mimic precision hardware luminescence.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry balance. Structural surfaces, dashboards, and enterprise modules employ calibrated radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`) that communicate engineered stability rather than playful informality.

- **Standard Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Default Cards)**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius.
- **Large Panels & Hero Canvas Modules (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Deep Modals & Spotlight Banners (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Interactive Badges, Metric Indicators, and Inline Chips**: Full pill form (`9999px`) to create an immediate structural contrast against rectangular architectural blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Subtle top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`). Hover transitions to `#1D4ED8` with a low-intensity luminous perimeter glow (`0 0 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action**: `#0F172A` surface with 1px `#1E293B` stroke and `#F8FAFC` text. On hover, the border shifts to `#3B82F6` and background shifts to `#172554`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action**: Transparent surface with `#94A3B8` text, shifting to `#FFFFFF` with a faint `#1E293B` background on hover.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#111C38` fill, 1px `#1E293B` border, and `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius.
- Interactive cards feature a hover transition: 1px border becomes `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`, and the surface subtly lifts using a gradient sweep (`linear-gradient(180deg, #152244 0%, #111C38 100%)`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input frames feature an obsidian `#0B0F19` ground with 1px `#1E293B` border and `0.5rem` radius. Text color is `#F8FAFC` with `#64748B` placeholder text.
- Focused state transitions the border to `#3B82F6` accompanied by an electric halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`).
- Checkboxes and radios utilize `#0F172A` backgrounds with `#2563EB` fills upon active confirmation.

### Badges & Chips
- Status indicators and skill taxonomy chips utilize a pill silhouette (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Styled as semi-transparent badge containers (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.1)`) accompanied by high-luminance text (`#3B82F6`) and fine hairline boundary outlines.
- Specialized "AI Solution" badges incorporate a gradient background stop between `#2563EB` and `#06B6D4`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Enterprise data rows are divided by crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` borders without heavy outer grid lines.
- Alternate zebra striping utilizes `#0A1128` and `#0E172E`. Hover states across table records trigger an active background tint (`#162244`).

### Specialized Domain Modules
- **Talent & Solution Profile Cards**: Two-part architecture featuring technical competency metrics positioned opposite candidate or practice-lead summaries, partitioned by a vertical `1px` slate divider.
- **Metric Highlight Tiles**: High-contrast KPI visualizers featuring large display figures in `#FFFFFF` with adjacent trend indicators highlighted in `#06B6D4` (positive) or `#EA580C` (critical/alert).
